# Project Scope — Patient MDM
## Problem Statement
A hospital network with 4 source systems has duplicate patient records causing billing errors.

## Goal
Build a Patient Master Data Hub with one Golden Record per patient.

## Source Systems
- Apollo EHR
- Medisoft
- Legacy HIS
- LabConnect

## Success Metrics
- Reduce duplicate rate below 5%
- Achieve DQ score above 85%
